# Human–AI Charter · Public Consultation
# 《人類—AI 憲章》公開徵詢

**Govern how AI capabilities are translated into real-world power, not intelligence itself.**
**治理 AI 能力轉化為現實權力的過程，而不是治理智慧本身。**

This repository hosts the open public consultation on the *Human–AI Charter (Public Consultation Draft)*. Each of the 14 consultation questions has its own discussion thread under **Issues**. The consultation remains open, with no closing date.

本專案是《人類—AI 憲章》（公開徵詢草案）的公開討論區。14 個徵詢議題各有一則討論串，請到 **Issues** 留言。徵詢持續開放，沒有截止日。

## The 14 questions 十四個議題

| Group 分組 | Questions 議題 |
|---|---|
| A. Governability 可治理 | G1 Capability is not authority · G2 Multi-agent delegation · G3 Governors are governed too · G4 Responsibility registers for high-risk AI *(candidate clause A)* |
| B. Risk control 風險可控 | R1 Verify boundaries, not lives · R2 Distributed high-risk action gates · R3 Human confirmation of irreversible actions · R4 Open weights, offline systems and non-cooperating actors |
| C. Room to develop 可發展 | D1 Govern power, not thought · D2 Certification cost and fair markets · D3 Cross-border recognition |
| D. Legitimacy and the future 正當性與未來 | L1 Who has the right to write this charter? · L2 If AI becomes another kind of intelligent subject · L3 A public forum for users *(candidate clause B)* |

## How to take part 如何參與

1. Open the issue for the question you want to discuss (e.g. **[G2]**) and comment: agree, disagree or add, with reasons.
   找到對應議題（例如 **[G2]**）留言：同意、反對或補充，並說明理由。
2. Counter-examples, real cases and alternative wording are especially welcome.
   特別歡迎反例、實際案例與替代條文。
3. For a new issue not covered by the 14 questions, open a new issue.
   14 題以外的新議題，請另開一則 issue。
4. Prefer email? Write to **humanaicharter@gmail.com**, quoting the question number.
   也可寄信至 **humanaicharter@gmail.com**，註明議題編號。

The author will summarise the main comments and major disagreements, and respond publicly in later versions.
作者會整理主要意見與重大分歧，並在後續版本中公開回應。

## Ground rules 討論規則

- Argue with reasons and evidence; criticise ideas, not people. 以理由與證據討論，對事不對人。
- Do not post personal data, or exploitable security details before a fix. 請勿張貼個人資料，或尚未修補的可利用安全漏洞細節。
- Comments in Chinese or English are both welcome. 中英文留言皆可。
